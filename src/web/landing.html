How it works

  1. Send bill photos. Munshi reads every line: item, quantity, price, batch and expiry.
  2. Send a 30-second voice note of what ran out or is left, in Kannada, Hindi or English.
  3. Reply OK to the order Munshi prepares on salesman day. Edit it if you like. It never goes without you.

Orders within your cash

Every order is capped at the budget you set, counting the udhaar you're likely to collect and the dues you owe.

Scheme check

“Buy 12, get 1 free” on something that sells 6 a month locks ₹600 for 65 days. Munshi says skip, or split it with a nearby shop.

Expiry rescue

Stock that will expire before it sells is flagged in rupees, with the date the distributor's return window closes.

Learns your words

“biscuit chhota”, “uppu packet”, “maggi bada”. It asks once, then remembers. At most three questions a day.

Start in two minutes

Scan with your phone camera, or tap the button. Munshi asks six short questions, then you send your first bills.

Open WhatsApp

Your shop's data stays yours. Bill photos are read by Claude and voice notes by Sarvam, only to run your shop's ledger; only anonymous area-level totals are ever shared. UPI SMS: amounts only, never names.
